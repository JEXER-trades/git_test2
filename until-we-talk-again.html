My love,

I know these forty days won't be easy. I'm going to miss your messages, your voice, and every little "good morning" more than I know how to say. But I want you to know that I understand, completely. Family comes first, and I would never want you to feel torn between them and me.

So go. Be there for them. Give them your time, your care, and your whole heart, the same way you've given it to me. That's one of the reasons I love you so much.

Please don't worry about me, and please don't feel guilty. Nothing about us changes because we're quiet for a while. My feelings don't need daily messages to stay alive. They're already part of me.

I made you this so that even on the days we can't talk, you'll still hear from me a little. There's one note waiting for you every day. Open them slowly. Let them keep you company.

And whenever this month is over, whether it's forty days or a little longer, I'll be right here. Same heart, same love, and probably missing you even more.

Take care of yourself. Eat well, sleep well (I know you will 😄), and remember that someone far away is thinking of you every single day.

Waiting for you, always 🤍
